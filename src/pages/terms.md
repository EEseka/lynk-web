## Splitting the bill

Payments are optional and are turned on by the host of a hangout.

### How it works

1. **The host enters the full cost** of the hangout, including their own share. Lynk divides it evenly between everyone going at the moment payments are turned on, host included. Each share must come to at least ₦1.
2. **The host sets a deadline.** Everyone else pays their share before it. The host's own share is not collected.
3. **Checkout is run by Paystack.** You pay on Paystack's own secure page, using the payment methods Paystack offers. Your card or bank details go to Paystack, never to Lynk. Paystack's terms also apply to your payment.
4. **You pay the share plus Paystack's processing fee.** The total is shown before you pay. Lynk does not add a fee of its own.
5. **Once everyone has paid,** the money collected is sent to the host's bank account through Paystack. How quickly it arrives depends on Paystack and the host's bank.

All amounts are in Nigerian naira.

### If the deadline passes

If some people have not paid by the deadline, the host decides what happens: remove the people who have not paid and go ahead with the money collected, or cancel the hangout, in which case everyone who paid is refunded. The host can also change or extend the deadline.

### Refunds

- Your share is **refunded automatically** if the hangout is cancelled, or if you leave it, **before the payout to the host has started.**
- A refund returns your share. **Paystack's processing fee is not refunded.**
- Refunds are sent back through Paystack. How long they take to reach you depends on Paystack and your bank.
- **Once the payout has started, Lynk cannot take the money back** from the host's account. Anything owed after that point is between you and the host.

### If you are the host

- You must give a bank account that is yours, or that you are allowed to receive money into, and keep it correct. We check the account with Paystack, but a payout sent to the account you gave counts as paid.
- The money you receive is your friends' contribution to the hangout. Use it for that.
- If a payout fails, we will tell you, and you can try again from the app.
- Once the payout has started, you can no longer cancel the hangout in the app. If plans change after that, settle it with your guests directly.
- You are responsible for any tax that applies to money you receive.
